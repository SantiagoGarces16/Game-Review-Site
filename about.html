About GameCritique

GameCritique is an independent gaming review hub created to compare video game ratings from critics and players in one place.

How Scores Are Calculated

Each overall score is calculated using three main factors:

40%

Metacritic Press Score

The average critical score from gaming publications and journalists around the world.

30%

Steam Player Reviews

The percentage of positive reviews from verified players on Steam.

30%

Editorial & Community Rating

Our own gameplay assessment combined with feedback from our readers.

Data Sources & Fair Use

GameCritique uses publicly available information like release dates, developers, and numerical review scores to provide fair comparisons of games. We do not copy full articles or reviews from other outlets.

We include direct links to official store pages and publisher websites so readers can find the original sources easily.
